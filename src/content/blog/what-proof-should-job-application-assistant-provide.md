**A job application assistant should provide enough evidence to identify the employer, role, requisition, source URL, submission time, status, and exact resume used.** Stronger proof also records important screening answers, confirmation details, exceptions, and corrections. An application count without job-level records is activity reporting, not evidence that suitable applications were completed accurately.

Proof protects both sides: the candidate can inspect the work, and the assistant can show what was actually done.

## The minimum application receipt

Every claimed submission should produce one application-level record containing:

| Field | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Employer and role | Identifies the opportunity |
| Requisition ID | Distinguishes similar or cross-posted openings |
| Canonical job URL | Points to the employer listing rather than a tracking link |
| Submission timestamp | Shows when the work occurred |
| Submission channel | Records the ATS, employer site, referral, board, or other route |
| Status | Separates prepared, submitted, failed, withdrawn, and needs-review work |
| Resume version | Shows the exact document associated with the application |
| Confirmation reference | Preserves an email, page, or employer portal result when available |

A row that says “50 applications completed” cannot reveal duplicates, wrong-fit jobs, failed submissions, or inconsistent documents. Ask for job-level detail.

## Match the proof level to the risk

Not every field needs a screenshot. Evidence should become more detailed as the answer becomes more consequential.

### Standard proof

Use structured records for stable facts and routine fields: contact information, work history copied from the approved profile, education, and the uploaded document.

### Important-answer proof

Retain the exact answer or a screenshot for questions about:

- Work authorization and sponsorship.
- Salary expectations.
- Willingness to relocate or travel.
- Security clearance and professional licenses.
- Employment restrictions and conflicts.
- Criminal-history or other legally sensitive questions where applicable.
- Candidate declarations and attestations.

### Exception proof

When an assistant pauses, skips, or asks the candidate, record the question, reason, decision, and person who approved it. An escalation log proves that the process did not quietly guess.

## Screenshots help, but they are not the whole record

A screenshot can show visible fields and a confirmation page. It can also expose personal data, omit hidden selections, or become difficult to search.

The best system combines structured data with selective screenshots:

- Structured fields make records searchable and comparable.
- Screenshots preserve context for important or unusual forms.
- The submitted file preserves the employer-facing document.
- A timestamped event log shows preparation, approval, submission, and failure.

Do not accept a screenshot folder with no index. Each image should be tied to one application and labeled by stage.

## Distinguish submitted, attempted, and completed

These statuses are not interchangeable:

1. **Prepared:** Documents or answers are ready, but nothing was sent.
2. **Attempted:** Work began, but a form error, expired job, login, assessment, or unanswered question prevented completion.
3. **Submitted:** The application reached the employer workflow and generated a reliable completion signal.
4. **Employer-confirmed:** An employer or ATS confirmation email, portal record, or reference is available.
5. **Needs candidate action:** An assessment, identity check, scheduling step, or sensitive decision remains.

A provider should not count attempts as successful applications. Ask how its headline total treats failures, duplicates, withdrawn jobs, and candidate-action steps.

## Proof should reveal application quality

Evidence is useful only when it can answer whether the work matched your rules. Audit a sample for:

- Correct role family and seniority.
- Allowed locations and working arrangements.
- Salary and employment-type fit.
- Required licenses, clearance, or work authorization.
- Excluded employers and industries.
- Correct source facts and resume policy.
- No duplicate application for the requisition.

Use the [wrong-job prevention guide](/blog/how-to-stop-auto-apply-tools-from-applying-to-wrong-jobs) and [duplicate application checklist](/blog/how-to-prevent-duplicate-job-applications) to evaluate those controls.

## Protect personal information inside the evidence

Application proof can contain addresses, phone numbers, immigration details, demographic responses, disability information, and account identifiers. More evidence is not automatically safer.

Ask the provider:

- Which fields are redacted from screenshots.
- Who can view the records.
- Whether links expire or require authentication.
- How long evidence is retained.
- How to export and delete it.
- Whether support staff or contractors can access it.

The candidate should receive useful accountability without creating an unsecured duplicate archive of sensitive information.

## How to audit the first batch

Review the first 10–20 applications before increasing volume. For each one, compare the job with the profile, inspect the resume, verify important answers, and confirm the recorded status.

Track:

- Qualified applications.
- Wrong-fit applications per 100.
- Duplicates prevented and duplicates sent.
- Applications needing correction.
- Records missing required proof.
- Candidate review time.
- Interviews per 100 qualified applications.

The audit is not about finding one cosmetic difference. It tests whether the operating process is reliable enough to continue.

## Questions to ask before paying

1. Can I export a job-level application log?
2. Is the exact submitted resume retained?
3. Which answers receive detailed evidence?
4. Are failed attempts separated from submissions?
5. How are confirmations recorded?
6. Can I see who approved an exception?
7. How are manual and referral applications included?
8. What is redacted from screenshots?
9. How long is evidence retained?
10. How quickly can the work be paused after an error?

If the provider cannot demonstrate a sample receipt before purchase, treat its application totals cautiously.
