**Scout is a done-for-you job application service with two ways to delegate the work.** Choose an AI Assistant for a clear, repeatable search or a dedicated Human Assistant when the search needs more judgment. In both cases, you define the target, Scout handles the application work, and your dashboard keeps a record of what was sent.

If you have wondered, “Can someone apply for jobs for me?” the answer is yes—but the process, controls, and evidence matter. Here is how Scout works and what powers it.

## The problem Scout exists to solve

A serious job search can consume hours of searching, tailoring, and form-filling every week. That is difficult to sustain, especially when you are employed, managing a career change, or already exhausted by the search.

Job seekers often respond in one of two ways: submit too few applications to build momentum, or send the same generic resume everywhere. Scout offers a third option: delegate the repetitive application work while keeping control of the search.

## What happens when you join

### 1. Build your profile once

Upload your resume and define the roles, locations, work arrangements, experience levels, and companies you want to target or exclude. Scout also collects the recurring facts application forms ask for. This becomes the operating brief for your assistant.

You can create separate job profiles when you are pursuing different role families. That keeps the search criteria and resume strategy for each track distinct.

### 2. Choose your assistant

The **AI Assistant** is the lower-cost lane. It matches jobs against your profile, prepares tailored resumes and cover notes where included, submits applications, and records the job, status, and resume used. It works best when your target titles and constraints are clear.

The **Human Assistant** assigns a real person to your search. Your assistant works from your profiles, handles applications, communicates with you in a private WhatsApp group, and provides detailed evidence of important form answers. It is a stronger fit for senior searches, career pivots, or applications that frequently require judgment.

### 3. Applications move through the queue

Scout is not a one-time resume blast. AI plans provide a recurring monthly or 90-day application allowance. Human plans are one-time bundles of 250, 500, or 1,000 applications, with standard bundles that do not expire and discounted bundles designed for a 90-day campaign.

Your assistant works within the plan, job profiles, and instructions you choose. You can also add a specific job from the dashboard or Scout browser extension and select the profile that should be used.

### 4. Track the work and take over at the interview

Your dashboard records the employer, role, application status, and resume used. Human Assistant plans also include screenshots or records of important application answers; the AI lane does not include those detailed form-answer screenshots.

Scout gets applications into employer pipelines. You remain responsible for interviews, assessments, recruiter conversations, and deciding which opportunity to pursue.

## AI Assistant or Human Assistant: how to choose

Choose the **AI Assistant** when your search can be expressed as stable rules: a defined group of titles, locations, seniority levels, work arrangements, and exclusions. It is designed for repeatable execution at a lower price.

Choose the **Human Assistant** when context changes the answer. A career pivot may require harder curation. A senior role may involve unusual forms or positioning. A multi-track campaign may need several profiles and closer coordination. Human plans add direct WhatsApp communication and detailed evidence of important answers.

Neither lane can guarantee interviews or a job. The value is consistent execution, truthful tailoring from your supplied information, and a record you can inspect—not a promise to control employer decisions.

## Questions people ask about how Scout works

### Will employers know Scout helped with an application?

Scout submits applications using the information and materials associated with your profile. Regardless of who completes the form, you remain responsible for ensuring those details are accurate. Scout is designed around matched jobs and tailored materials, not sending one unchanged resume indiscriminately.

### What do I still have to do?

You set the search direction, provide accurate information, answer questions that require your judgment, and handle interviews, assessments, and employer communication. Scout handles the repeatable application operations.

### Can I see what was submitted?

Both lanes record the job, status, and resume used. Human Assistant plans add detailed evidence or screenshots of important form answers. AI Assistant plans do not include those detailed screenshots.

### What happens when I pause or finish a plan?

An AI automation can be paused, which stops new automated work until it is activated again. AI billing follows the recurring plan you select. Human Assistant bundles are one-time purchases and the application work ends when the bundle is used; standard bundles do not expire, while discounted 90-day bundles must be used within that period.

### Can Scout apply to a job I find myself?

Yes. Add the job from your dashboard or the Scout browser extension, then choose the job profile or resume strategy your assistant should follow.
